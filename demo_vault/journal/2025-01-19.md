Went hiking at Bukit Tabur with Jun this morning. We started at 6 and reached the ridge just as the sun came up over the reservoir. It was worth the early start.

My knees hurt on the way down and I was slow on the rocky part. Jun waited without complaining, which I appreciated.

I felt calm for the first time in weeks. I want to do this once a month.
