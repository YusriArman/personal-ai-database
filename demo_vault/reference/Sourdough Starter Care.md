# Sourdough Starter Care

How to keep the starter alive.

## Feeding

Feed at a ratio of 1:1:1 by weight: one part starter, one part flour, one part water. At room temperature feed every 24 hours. In the fridge, feed once a week.

## Signs

- Ready to use when it has doubled and a spoonful floats in water.
- A grey liquid on top means it is hungry. Pour it off and feed.
- Pink or orange streaks mean it has gone bad. Throw it away.

## Flour

Wholemeal flour makes it rise faster than white flour.
