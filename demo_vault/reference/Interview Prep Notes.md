# Interview Prep Notes

## Behavioural questions

Use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep the situation short and spend most of the time on the action.

## Common questions

- Tell me about a project you are proud of.
- Describe a time you disagreed with a teammate.
- What would you do differently?

## Technical

Talk through the approach before writing code. State the time and space complexity without being asked. Test with an empty input.

## Questions to ask them

What does a typical week look like for an intern, and who would I learn from?
