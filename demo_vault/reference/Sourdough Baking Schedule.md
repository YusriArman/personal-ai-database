# Sourdough Baking Schedule

Timeline for one loaf, assuming an active starter (see [[Sourdough Starter Care]]).

## Day one

- 9 am: mix flour and water, rest 30 minutes.
- 9:30 am: add starter and salt.
- Bulk fermentation takes about 5 hours at 26 °C, with a fold every 45 minutes for the first two hours.
- 3 pm: shape, then into the fridge overnight.

## Day two

- Heat the oven and the pot to 230 °C.
- Bake 20 minutes with the lid on, then 25 minutes with the lid off.
- Wait at least one hour before cutting.
