# Greenhouse Monitor

A small box that watches the herb trays in the club greenhouse and warns us before the soil dries out. It runs on a Raspberry Pi Pico W and posts readings every ten minutes.

## Parts and budget

Total budget is RM180, paid from club funds. The capacitive soil probes were the expensive part at RM22 each. Wiring follows [[Raspberry Pi Pico Pinout]].

## Status

Readings were noisy until I redid the calibration, see [[Sensor Calibration Log]]. The demo is on 14 March in front of the faculty panel.
