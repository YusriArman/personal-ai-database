# Budget Tracker App

A phone app for logging daily spending, built with Flutter and a local SQLite database. No account, no sync, everything stays on the phone.

## Features so far

Categories, a monthly summary screen and a pie chart of spending per category. Recurring expenses such as rent are entered once and repeat on their own.
