# Campus Map Dataset

A CSV of GPS coordinates for campus buildings, collected by walking around with my phone. There are 37 buildings so far, each with a name, latitude, longitude and number of floors.

## Why

The official campus map is a picture, so nobody can build anything on top of it. Jun wants to use the data for a shortest-path assignment.

## Quality

Phone GPS is off by up to 8 metres near the tall engineering block. I averaged three readings per building to reduce the error.
